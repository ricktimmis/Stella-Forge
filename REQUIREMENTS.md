# PROJECT REQUIREMENTS: STELLA FORGE (Requirements derived from VISION.md)

## 📝 Overview
This document outlines the system and operational requirements necessary to fulfill the Stella Forge vision. All requirements are classified by priority (P1: Critical for MVP/Grant; P2: Strategic Enhancement; P3: Future Scope).

---

## 🚀 1. OPERATIONAL & TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS (The "How")
*Focus: Building the capability for fabrication and logistics.*

**P1: Core Fabrication Capability**
*   **REQ-TEC-001:** The system must demonstrate the ability to fabricate a functional, complex structural component (e.g., LEO relay module, Lunar habitat segment) using in-situ resources or a defined set of initial payloads.
*   **REQ-TEC-002:** The manufacturing process must be semi-autonomous, requiring minimal direct human intervention once initiated (Autonomous Operation Rating > 0.75).
*   **REQ-TEC-003:** The manufacturing capability must be deployable to a target environment: Earth Orbit (LEO/GEO) or Lunar proximity.

**P2: Logistics and Supply Chain**
*   **REQ-TEC-004:** The system must model and simulate a closed-loop logistics pathway between Earth, an orbital staging point, and a designated target environment.
*   **REQ-TEC-005:** The system must define and adhere to strict payload mass and volume constraints for all inter-planetary movements.

**P3: Scalability and Durability**
*   **REQ-TEC-006:** The implemented technologies must be validated for long-term operation (minimum 5-year simulated lifespan).

## 💼 2. COMMERCIAL & GOVERNANCE REQUIREMENTS (The "Why Invest?")
*Focus: Meeting funding criteria and proving market viability.*

**P1: Funding and Grant Compliance**
*   **REQ-CMR-001 (Grant):** The project must produce verifiable data points demonstrating the potential reduction of launch mass/cost vs. Earth-centric manufacturing (required for UK Government Grant applications).
*   **REQ-CMR-002 (Investment):** The project must generate a complete, defensible Pitch Deck and Business Model Canvas outlining clear paths to revenue and market capture (required for Seed Round).
*   **REQ-CMR-003:** A formal **Adversarial Risk Register** must be maintained, documenting the strongest critique against the core technological premise.

**P2: Market Position**
*   **REQ-CMR-004:** The project must clearly define its unique competitive advantage over existing terrestrial or nascent space-based manufacturing solutions.
*   **REQ-CMR-005:** The project must outline initial strategic partnership targets (e.g., governmental space agencies, large aerospace primes).

## 🌐 3. SCIENTIFIC & ENVIRONMENTAL REQUIREMENTS (The "What")
*Focus: Establishing technical feasibility.*

**P1: Resource Utilization**
*   **REQ-SCI-001:** The system must define validated processes (or theoretical pathways) for In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU) using local or targeted resources (e.g., lunar regolith, orbital materials).
*   **REQ-SCI-002:** All scientific hypotheses underlying the fabrication method must be clearly documented and validated by simulation data sets.
